## 2. Moderation rules

| Action | Applies to |
|---|---|
| **Hide** | Spam, scams, and comments containing slurs |
| **Delete** | Targeted harassment of an identifiable person; anything that identifies a private individual as responsible |
| **Report** | Threats, doxxing, coordinated spam |
| **Leave, no reply** | Hostility, bad-faith arguments, "this is boring," off-topic politics |
| **Always reply** | Factual questions, family connections, good-faith disagreement |

We never delete legitimate criticism. We never argue. We never edit someone's comment.

## 3. Correction protocol

If a posted caption or image contains a material error:

1. Fix it within 24 hours.
2. Post a visible note: `Correction:` followed by what was wrong and what is correct.
3. Log it in `14-postmortem.md`.
4. If the error is structural (wrong event, wrong death toll, wrong attribution), unpublish and rebuild.

Two known risks to watch: anyone repeating the "812" death toll, and anyone asserting a criminal
conviction. Both are addressed in the reply bank.

## 4. Escalation

- **Factual dispute we cannot resolve** → `standards-editor`, then `research-journalist`.
- **Rights complaint about an image** → `standards-editor` immediately; the post may be taken down
  while it is checked.
- **Media or institutional inquiry** → `agency-lead`.
- **A family member asking us to change something** → `agency-lead` and `standards-editor` together.
  We take it seriously.

## 5. First 24 hours

A human should watch for:

- The first hour of comments, when the reply bank's factual answers matter most.
- Any family connection, which should be answered by a person, not a template.
- Any correction request, which is handled before anything else.
- Whether the hook is holding — the ratio of saves to reach in the first three hours.
