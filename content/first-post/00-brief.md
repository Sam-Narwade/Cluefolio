## 1. The assignment

One complete, publication-ready image-led post for Instagram and Facebook, aimed at a US audience.
No monthly calendar. This is Post 001 and it must establish the studio's standard.

## 2. The angle

> A steamship capsized while still tied to its dock, killing 844 people in twenty feet of water,
> twenty feet from shore — and nobody was ever held responsible.

## 3. Why this story, why now

- The central fact is a contradiction strong enough to stop a scroll: the ship never left the dock.
- It is a genuine engineering-failure story: top-weight, stability, and a safety law that made a
  known flaw worse. That makes it relevant to modern readers, not just history buffs.
- Primary sources are abundant and free: NARA, the Library of Congress, congressional records.
- It is widely under-known relative to its scale — 844 deaths, the deadliest wreck in Great Lakes
  history — which gives it "I never knew this" share value.
- Authentic public-domain photography exists, so no AI reconstruction is needed and no disclosure
  is required.

## 4. Non-negotiable facts

These are verified before writing and may not change without a new fact-check:

- The Eastland capsized on July 24, 1915, in the Chicago River, while still moored to the wharf.
- 844 passengers and crew died.
- The water at the site was about 20 feet deep; the ship lay roughly 20 feet from the dock wall.
- The ship had a documented history of severe listing before the disaster.
- The dead included a large number of young people and multiple entire families.
- Criminal prosecutions ended without a conviction.
- The ship was later raised, renamed USS *Wilmette*, and served into the 1940s.

## 5. Audience

US adults 25–55 who follow history, engineering failures, and true-story accounts on Instagram and
Facebook. They save and share posts that teach them something specific they did not know. They
dislike being manipulated and they notice errors.

## 6. Platforms and format

- Instagram: 4:5, 1080×1350, caption ≤2,200 characters, 4–6 hashtags.
- Facebook: 4:5, 1080×1350, platform-native caption, 3–4 hashtags.
- Image count: six. Determined by the Art Director after the outline (see `08-visual-plan.md`).

## 7. Known risks

| Risk | Mitigation | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Death-toll figures differ between sources (844 vs 812) | Use the official figure, note the Coroner's figure is disputed in the ledger | `fact-checker` |
| Competing accounts of the legal outcome | Verify precisely; if unresolved, describe only what is certain | `fact-checker` |
| Sensitivity of a mass-casualty story | No corpse or body-recovery imagery; no lingering on suffering | `standards-editor` |
| Period handwriting on one archival photo carries a wrong date | Crop it out of the final frame | `art-director` |
| Archival scans vary in quality | Unified grade and grain; reject any frame that cannot hold 1080 px | `image-producer` |
