## Handling rules for disputed and narrow claims

- **C02** — We state 844 as the death toll. A widely circulated figure of 812 is unsupported and does
  not appear anywhere in the caption or the ledger as a competing toll.
- **C12** — The criminal cases ended in acquittal in February 1916 and no trial was held in Chicago.
  We state that plainly and name no one as convicted. The separate 1933 **civil** ruling is not
  mentioned in the caption.
- **C18** — Only used in its narrow, congressional form ("civilian passengers"). Never shortened to
  "more than the *Titanic*." In fact it is not used in this caption at all.
- **Crew deaths** — No number is stated. The dead are described as "passengers and crew."
