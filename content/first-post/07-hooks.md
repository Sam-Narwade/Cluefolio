## Rejections

- **#4** overpromises. The law is one contributing factor, not a cause, and a one-line hook cannot
  carry that qualification. It would be the most shareable line and the least honest.
- **#8** presumes the reader's location. False for a national audience.
- **#9** is generic and tells the reader nothing.
- **#10** is banned by the rubric: vague, manufactured, and exactly the voice we refuse.

---

## Recommendation

**Primary (Instagram and Facebook):**
> The Eastland was still tied to the dock when it killed 844 people.

**Rationale:** two short sentences, a number in the first line, a contradiction the story pays off
immediately, and the cover image supplies the ship without repeating the words.

**Secondary / alt text opener:**
> 844 people died in twenty feet of water, twenty feet from shore.

**Rationale:** the physical impossibility of the geography is the most durable and least
sensational detail in the story. It works as a standalone line if the primary hook is ever retired.

---

## Pairing check

The cover photograph shows the ship on its side. The hook says it never left the dock. The text and
the image each carry a different half of the contradiction — neither is redundant. Scored 4/5 on
visual pairing for exactly that reason.
