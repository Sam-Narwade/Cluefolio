## Candidate A — Hindenburg disaster, Lakehurst, New Jersey, 1937

> The Hindenburg caught fire and fell from the sky while coming in for a landing, killing 36 people in thirty-four seconds.

| Criterion | Score | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Hook strength | 5 | "Caught fire and fell from the sky" is a genuine visual contradiction |
| Evidence quality | 5 | NARA, Wikimedia Commons, aviation archives, multiple independent sources |
| Emotional impact | 5 | Mass casualty, survival stories, iconic image; handled with restraint |
| Visual potential | 5 | Sam Shere photograph: the airship coming in, then engulfed in flames |
| Narrative depth | 5 | Design, lifting gas, weather, ground handling, investigation, survivor accounts |
| US relevance | 5 | US soil (New Jersey); American aviation history; national memory |
| Originality | 4 | Well documented but under-known details of the photograph and final moments |
| Share/save potential | 5 | "I never knew the cause" plus iconic image worth saving |

**Total** | **39/40** | 

**Angle:** The Hindenburg was coming in for a landing at Lakehurst when it caught fire and fell from the sky — 36 people died in thirty-four seconds, and the official cause was never proven.

**Verdict:** SELECTED.
