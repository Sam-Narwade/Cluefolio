## Handling rules for disputed and narrow claims

- **C09** — We state the cause as "most likely leaking hydrogen ignited by static electricity" and qualify it as the current best explanation, not a proven fact. We do not present it as the official determined cause.
- **C16** — We note that rainy conditions were reported but do not emphasize them as a confirmed cause. We describe the approach as occurring "in existing weather conditions."
- **Death toll** — We state 35 on board (13 passengers, 22 crew) + 1 ground crew member killed = 36 total. We distinguish between those on board and the ground fatality.
