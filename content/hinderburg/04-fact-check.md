## 2. The material errors found

No material errors were found in the draft research. All claims have been verified or appropriately qualified. The one caveat is C09: the hydrogen-and-static theory is the most likely explanation per current forensic analysis, but it remains unproven. The fact-check confirms this qualification is handled correctly in the source ledger and will be reflected in the caption.
