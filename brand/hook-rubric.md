# Hook Rubric

The hook is the first line of the caption and the first frame of the carousel. It has one job:
make a specific promise the story keeps.

## The three-second test

A scrolling user sees roughly 125 characters and one image. In three seconds they decide.

Ask:
1. Is there a concrete noun or number in the first six words?
2. Is there a contradiction, a stake, or an unanswered question?
3. Does it avoid every banned opening in `voice.md`?
4. Does the image add a second, non-redundant piece of information?

## Hook patterns that work for us

### The impossible fact
> The ship never left the dock.

### The number with a physical anchor
> 844 people died in twenty feet of water, twenty feet from shore.

### The contradiction
> A brand-new safety law helped kill 844 people.

### The quiet image
> At 7:28 on a July morning, a 265-foot steamship rolled onto its side in downtown Chicago.

### The delayed consequence
> The captain survived. He was one of the last people to walk off his own ship.

## Scoring a hook (1–5 each)

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Specificity | Vague | Some detail | Concrete noun + number + place |
| Tension | None | Mild curiosity | Genuine unanswered question |
| Payoff honesty | Overpromises | Roughly fair | Exactly what the story delivers |
| Visual pairing | Image repeats text | Image supports text | Image and text add different information |
| Voice fit | Generic | Acceptable | Unmistakably Cluefolio |

**20 max.** Ship at ≥16. Below 16, rewrite, do not ship.

## What we never do

- Withhold the death toll to force a click.
- Use "you won't believe" or "wait for it."
- Promise a mystery the story resolves in the first paragraph.
- Put a question mark where a fact belongs.
- Use a corpse or a grieving face as the thumbnail.

## Worked example

**Weak:** "This forgotten disaster shocked Chicago. 🧵"
Why: vague, no specifics, engagement bait, emoji as decoration.

**Better:** "844 people died on a ship that never left the dock."
Why: number, contradiction, honest.

**Best:** "The ship never left the dock. 844 people died anyway."
Why: two short sentences, a real contradiction, and the image supplies the ship.
